Taxi 1 (1998)
  • Director: Gérard Pirès
  • Actors: Samy Naceri, Frédéric Diefenthal, Marion Cotillard, Bernard Farcy, Emma Wiklund
  • Year | Country: 1998 | France
  • Duration: 1:25:15

Description:

Daniel, a reckless former pizza delivery man, finally realizes his dream and becomes a taxi driver. His hot white Peugeot is capable of Formula One speeds, and Daniel hates the police. But when he's accidentally caught speeding by clumsy cop Emilien, he's faced with a stark choice: Either he loses his license or helps catch a gang of elusive German robbers in red Mercedes! The duo of the year begins their jet-setting ride through the streets of Marseille!
